Most AI agents are amnesiacs. You give them a production outage at 2 AM, and they hallucinate dangerous textbook advice because they don't remember yesterday.

When a payment timeout hit our cluster, a stateless LLM suggested restarting the worker pods. That restart wiped in-flight locks and caused a 48-minute cascading outage.

The fix? Giving the agent long-term memory with Hindsight.

Instead of generic guesses, the agent:
1. Recalled an identical outage from 18 days ago in sub-100ms
2. Flagged the deadly restart antipattern before the engineer ran it
3. Surfaced the exact verified patch script, dropping MTTR to 34s

Memory shouldn't just be vector chunks. It needs entity graphs, causality, and reflections over time.

Project repo: https://github.com/Md-Abdul-Kareem/siterecall
