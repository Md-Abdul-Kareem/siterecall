Imagine visiting a doctor who forgets your medical history every 5 minutes and prescribes the wrong medicine.

That is how most AI works today during a website outage.

When an online payment system broke on a busy evening, standard AI said: "Just restart all the servers!"

That advice was a disaster. Restarting wiped active customer transactions, charged people twice, and turned a small glitch into a 48-minute outage.

Why? Because the AI had zero memory that an engineer had already solved this exact problem 18 days ago without restarting anything.

To fix this, we built SiteRecall using Google Antigravity, Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Hindsight.

With persistent memory, the AI:
1. Remembered the past outage in 0.1 seconds
2. Warned: "DO NOT restart servers — it drops payments"
3. Handed the engineer the verified 34-second safe fix

When AI remembers the past, it stops repeating painful mistakes.
